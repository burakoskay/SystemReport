## Uber Scales Back AI Spending

Uber has capped employee AI spending after depleting its annual budget in just four months.[^1][^2][^3][^4] The company's president, Andrew Macdonald, expressed skepticism about the return on investment, citing a lack of clear connection between AI usage and productivity.[^3][^5][^6][^7]

The company's AI spending spree occurred after reportedly encouraging staff to use AI as much as possible. However, Macdonald told Rapid Response that the link between token consumption for Claude Code and delivering more useful features to consumers is not clear.[^3][^5][^8] "That link is not there yet, right? I think maybe implicitly there is more that is getting shipped, but it’s very hard to draw a line between one of those stats and, ‘Okay, now we’re actually producing 25 percent more useful consumer features,’" he said.[^3][^5][^8][^6]

## Financials and Strategy

Uber spent $3.4 billion on research and development efforts in 2025, 9 percent more than the previous year.[^3] To offset increasing AI investments, Uber CEO Dara Khosrowshahi said the company is hiring fewer human employees. Macdonald emphasized the need to justify AI spending: "We’re going to have to start talking about token consumption and the associated cost versus headcount. So if you’re not actually able to draw a direct line to how much useful features and functionality you’re shipping to your users, that trade becomes harder to justify."[^5]
