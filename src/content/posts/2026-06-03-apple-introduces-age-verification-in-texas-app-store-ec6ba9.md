## Age Verification Hits Texas
Apple starts age verification in the Texas App Store on June 4th, following a federal appeals court decision allowing Texas' App Store Accountability Act to take effect.
New Apple account holders in Texas must verify their age as over 18 using a credit card or a verification service.
## The Law Behind the Move
The Texas App Store Accountability Act aims to regulate app use by minors, requiring app stores to verify users' ages and obtain parental consent for minors.
Apple's age verification move complies with this law, focusing on confirming users are over 18.
## Impact on Users
For new users in Texas, age verification adds an extra step when creating an Apple account, using a credit card or a verification service.
## Industry Context
The Texas App Store Accountability Act's impact on the tech industry will be significant, particularly for app developers catering to younger audiences, who must adapt to the new regulations.

## Technical Mechanics
From a technical perspective, Apple's age verification system relies on a combination of credit card information and government ID verification. The system is designed to ensure that users are who they claim to be and that they are over 18.
This is achieved through a secure online process that verifies the user's identity and age. The process is mandatory for all new Apple account holders in Texas.
