## Soaring AI Costs Hit Corporate America

Microsoft and Walmart are among companies limiting AI usage due to high costs. The Wall Street Journal reports that businesses are reining in AI spending as costs mount. This shift is a clear indication that the initial excitement around AI adoption is giving way to a more pragmatic approach, where companies must balance the benefits of AI with its financial implications.

The decision to limit AI usage is not taken lightly, as companies weigh the benefits of AI against its costs. For instance, AI can automate routine tasks, freeing up employees to focus on more strategic work. However, the cost of AI models can be prohibitively expensive, especially for small and medium-sized businesses.

## The Cost of AI

Companies like Coca-Cola are prioritizing AI projects, while others are forced to make tough choices. The cost of AI is not just a matter of the initial investment but also the ongoing expenses associated with its usage. As AI models become more complex and data-intensive, their costs are skyrocketing, making it challenging for companies to manage their AI expenses.

The cost of AI can be broken down into several components, including the cost of training and deploying models, as well as the cost of maintaining and updating them. Companies must also consider the cost of data storage and processing, which can add up quickly.
