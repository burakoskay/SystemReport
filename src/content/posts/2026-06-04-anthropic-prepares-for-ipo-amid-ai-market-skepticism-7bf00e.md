Anthropic co-founder Daniela Amodei said the company will proceed with its initial public offering despite concerns over AI returns. Amodei stated that token-maxxing criticism will not impact the IPO plan. She noted that the public market could provide the necessary capital to scale Anthropic's models.

## Anthropic's IPO Push and Market Skepticism
Anthropic's leadership views the IPO as a response to investor demand for generative AI. Amodei emphasized that the company's revenue pipeline supports a public listing. She disputed the notion that token-maxxing concerns pose a material risk.

Critics argue that AI valuations are volatile. Other AI firms have seen share prices drop after lofty forecasts. The difference between Anthropic's confidence and market caution sets a tone for the coming months.
