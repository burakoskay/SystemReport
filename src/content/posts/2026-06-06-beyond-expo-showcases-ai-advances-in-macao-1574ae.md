## BEYOND Expo Highlights AI and Tech Innovations

The 6th BEYOND Expo concluded its 'AI: Digital to Physical' technology showcase in Macao, featuring the latest products, innovative tech, thought leadership, and industry debate. The event drew over 30,000 visitors from more than 120 countries to see nearly 800 companies exhibit their products.

The expo included humanoids for industry, retail, and the home, as well as advancements in aviation, education, food, healthcare, marine, sports, smart homes, and wearables. With a focus on Asian tech, 400 speakers shared their perspectives on tech-related topics via keynote presentations and panel discussions.

## Keynote Highlights and Startup Funding

At the BEYOND Expo Closing Ceremony, Kun Gao, Founder of Crunchyroll, discussed common problems faced by tech startups and encouraged founders to think beyond their technology to attract investors and partners. The final day included BEYOND AI Day, featuring Demo Hack Day presentations and the first ClawCon Macao.

The Closing Ceremony also included the finals of Fund at First Pitch (FAFP), where six finalists were judged and given company valuations by prominent investors. Innovative products included smart jewelry, wearables, a smart canoe paddle, and an emotion-sensitive hugging bear.

## Notable Products and Awards

The expo featured several notable products, including Aerofugia's production aircraft and eVTOL battery, GEMO's glass skin, iFlyTek's AI smart glasses, and Zero Zero Robotics' HOVERAir AQUA. The 'BEYOND Best of Innovation' awards were presented to 37 companies, including AEROFUGIA, AI Robotics, and iFLYTEK.
