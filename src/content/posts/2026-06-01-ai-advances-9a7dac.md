## NVIDIA's New Chips and Platforms
NVIDIA has announced several new developments, including the Isaac Gr00t platform for humanoid robotics and the RTX Spark chip for AI computing. The Isaac Gr00t platform is designed to give researchers access to frontier humanoid robotics, combining a humanoid robot with five-fingered hands and NVIDIA Jetson Thor compute.

The RTX Spark chip is an AI 'superchip' that will power Windows laptops and desktops. NVIDIA claims that the chip will offer 1 petaflop of AI computing power, making it a significant development in the field of AI computing. The implications of this chip are far-reaching, with potential applications in fields such as healthcare and finance.

## AI in Healthcare and Education
AI is also being used in healthcare and education. A new encryption framework allows hospitals to train medical foundation models together without sharing raw patient data, enhancing patient privacy.
