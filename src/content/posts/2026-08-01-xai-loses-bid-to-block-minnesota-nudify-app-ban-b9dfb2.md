## Introduction to the Ban
A judge has denied xAI's request to block a Minnesota ban on 'nudify' apps. This decision allows Minnesota to enforce the ban on apps that enable users to digitally remove clothing from images[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5].
## Background on the Decision
The court's ruling follows a lawsuit by xAI[^1][^6][^7][^8]. According to the ruling, Minnesota can now move forward with the ban[^1][^2][^4][^5]. The ban is part of a broader effort to regulate apps that provide 'nudify' capabilities, which have raised concerns about user safety and digital consent[^1][^2][^4][^5][^7].
## Implications of the Ban
