## A New Tier for Power Users

Meta has officially launched Instagram Plus, a paid subscription that gives users access to extra features, including tools for reaching wider or more specific audiences. For $3.99/month, subscribers will get a range of new features designed to help creators and heavy users grow their following and understand their audience.

The new features include the ability to see how many people have rewatched your Story in aggregate, create unlimited audience lists for Stories, spotlight a story once a week for additional views, extend a story beyond 24 hours, and preview a story without showing up as a viewer. Users will also be able to post straight to their profile and highlight without showing up on their followers' feeds.

## Features and Benefits

Instagram Plus offers a range of features that are designed to better serve creators and those looking to grow their following. These include Super Heart animated reactions for Stories, custom app icons, customizable fonts for profile bios, and access to additional pins for your profile. The subscription is tailored to each individual app, with Facebook Plus and Instagram Plus focused more on social expression, while WhatsApp Plus focuses on personalization and messaging.

The new subscription plans are separate from Meta Verified, which is focused on verification, impersonation protection, and extra support. Meta says it will begin testing other offerings, including professional plans for creators and businesses, and AI-focused plans for all users.

## Industry Context

The launch of Instagram Plus is part of a broader trend towards paid subscriptions on social media platforms. Snap's Snapchat+ offering, which starts at $3.99 per month, has topped 16 million subscribers, more than doubling since early 2024. Meta's new subscription plans are designed to provide additional features for power users who want more from their social apps, while also diversifying the company's revenue streams beyond advertising.

The company is taking a two-fold approach to its AI agent, Manus, integrating it into Meta products while continuing to sell standalone subscriptions to businesses. Meta plans to test subscriptions for AI features, such as Vibes video generation, which lets people create and remix AI-generated videos.

## History of Paid Subscriptions on Social Media

The concept of paid subscriptions on social media is not new. In the past, platforms like Twitter and YouTube have experimented with paid features and subscriptions. However, the current trend towards paid subscriptions on social media platforms began to take shape in 2020, with the launch of Twitter's Blue subscription service.

Since then, other platforms have followed suit, including Snapchat, which launched its Snapchat+ subscription service in 2022. The success of these subscription services has led to a surge in interest from other social media platforms, including Meta, which has now launched its own paid subscription service, Instagram Plus.

## Technical Mechanics

The technical side of Instagram Plus allows creators to pinpoint exactly who sees their stories. For the cost of $3.99 a month, they can see who doesn't follow back, and gain insights into their audience engagement. This feature is particularly useful for creators who want to understand their audience better and tailor their content accordingly.

The subscription service also allows users to customize their profiles with additional features such as custom app icons and customizable fonts for profile bios. These features are designed to give users more control over their online presence and help them stand out from the crowd.
