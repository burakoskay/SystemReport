## Disney+ Launches Short-Form Video Feed
Disney+ is launching a short-form video feed, partnering with TikTok creators to bring fan content to its app. This move is a response to the growing competition between streamers and social media giants.

The partnership allows Disney+ to tap into TikTok's creator network, featuring popular streamers and social media personalities. Disney+ will feature creator content on its short-form video feed, aiming to create a more engaging experience for its users.

Disney+'s decision to enter the short-form video space is a strategic move. By partnering with TikTok creators, Disney+ aims to attract a younger demographic, a crucial step in expanding its user base. The platform must carefully curate the content featured on its short-form video feed, ensuring it aligns with Disney+'s values and appeals to its target audience.
