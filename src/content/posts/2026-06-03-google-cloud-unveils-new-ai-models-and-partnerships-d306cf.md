## Google Cloud Bolsters AI Offerings with New Models and Features

Google Cloud has unveiled a series of updates to its artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities, including new models, expanded partnerships, and enhanced features for its Vertex AI platform. These updates aim to make AI more accessible and useful for businesses, allowing them to build and deploy AI-powered applications more efficiently.

The company has introduced Gemma 4 12B, a new model designed to run on laptops with 16GB of RAM, and Gemini 1.5 Pro, which is now available in public preview. Gemini 1.5 Pro boasts a 1 million-token context window, enabling multimodal reasoning over large amounts of data. Additionally, Google Cloud has updated its Imagen 2.0 model, which can create short, 4-second live images from text prompts.

Google Cloud's use of deep learning algorithms and natural language processing enables its AI models to learn from large datasets and improve over time, providing businesses with more accurate and effective AI-powered solutions.

## Enhanced Partnerships and Integrations

Google Cloud has also expanded its partnerships with various companies, including Lovable, which has signed a multiyear deal to increase its usage of Google Cloud by 5x. The company is also integrating its Google Developer Program premium benefits, including $10 monthly Google Cloud credits, directly into its Google AI Pro and Ultra subscriptions.
