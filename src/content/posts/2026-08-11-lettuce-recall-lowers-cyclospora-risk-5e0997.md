## Lettuce Safety Update
A professor of food safety says the removal of a contaminated product from the market has reduced the risk of cyclospora infection from eating lettuce. "That product is off the market, so I think there’s absolutely lower risk for cyclospora in terms of eating lettuce," the professor tells WIRED.
## Cyclospora Risk
Cyclospora is a parasite that can cause foodborne illness, particularly problematic for people with weakened immune systems. The professor notes that the risk of cyclospora infection is now lower due to the product recall. Cyclospora infections can cause a range of symptoms including diarrhea, weight loss, and stomach cramps. People with weakened immune systems are more susceptible to severe illness.
