## Speedex Tools Expands into Abu Dhabi

Speedex Tools, a leading UAE retailer and distributor of tools, hardware, and home improvement solutions, has officially opened its newest retail destination in Musaffah, Abu Dhabi. The new store introduces a customer-first retail concept designed for professionals, contractors, businesses, and homeowners seeking dependable tools, equipment, and expert guidance.

The store spans more than 10,000 sq ft and offers 20,000+ products across over twelve categories, supported by a curated portfolio of trusted international brands. A key highlight of the store is the dedicated Yato Garage setup, designed to showcase a fully equipped workshop environment featuring garage and workshop equipment in a real-world setting. This strategic location in one of Abu Dhabi's busiest commercial zones marks another milestone in the company's continued UAE expansion and its long-term commitment to serving the capital's growing trade, industrial, and home improvement sectors.

## AI-Powered Photography Platform Launches

Chase Kerlin and Michael Bashour, co-founders of HookPic, have developed an AI-powered photography platform that solves numerous pain points for manufacturers and retailers. The platform allows customers to create lifestyle photography, product silos, and marketing content from simple product photos, eliminating issues such as missed launches, inconsistent images, and budgets blown on a single photoshoot. Describing themselves as 'furniture people who finally had the tools to solve our own problem,' the co-founders developed a technology that enables their customers to produce high-quality content efficiently.

HookPic has already partnered with various companies in the furniture industry and continues to expand its capabilities, adding video elements and more. The platform's ease of use and capabilities of AI continue to develop, with new features added on an almost weekly basis. This innovation has the potential to significantly impact the way manufacturers and retailers approach product photography and marketing.
