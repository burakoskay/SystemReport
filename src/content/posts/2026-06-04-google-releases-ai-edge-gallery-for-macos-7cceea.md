## Google brings Gemma models to macOS
Google released the AI Edge Gallery for macOS, enabling local execution of Gemma models on Mac hardware. The rollout includes the Gemma 4 12B model and the AI Edge Eloquent dictation app.

The launch arrives as Apple begins a new advertising push for Safari’s privacy features. The 'Privacy on iPhone' campaign frames Safari as a shield that keeps data trackers off users’ backs.

## Apple's 'Privacy on iPhone' campaign
Apple's campaign highlights Safari's privacy-focused features and how it keeps data trackers off users' backs. The campaign targets iPhone users who care about data control.

## Google's AI Edge Gallery
Google’s AI Edge Gallery packages the Gemma family of models for direct use on Mac laptops and desktops. Users can download the Gemma 4 12B model and run inference without sending data to the cloud.

## Developer response and competing moves
Early feedback on Hacker News shows developers are curious about the performance of Gemma 4 12B on Apple Silicon. Comments note that the model’s size may be prohibitive for everyday desktop use but appreciate the ability to experiment without cloud credits.

## Technical Mechanics
The Gemma 4 12B model is a significant release, offering improved performance and efficiency compared to earlier models. The model's size and complexity require significant computational resources, making it a challenging task to run locally on Mac hardware.
