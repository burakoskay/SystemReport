## Introduction
Google lets users remove visible watermarks from its AI-generated content. This change is part of Google's ongoing effort to refine its AI generation tools. The visible watermark was initially added to distinguish AI-generated content from human-created work. Users can now opt out of displaying this watermark. Turning off this setting won't affect the invisible benchmarks used to identify AI-generated files.
## The Impact on AI-Generated Content
Removing visible watermarks may increase the use of AI-generated content. Without the visible watermark, AI-generated images or text may be more easily shared and used without revealing their artificial origin. Invisible benchmarks will still be present to help identify AI-generated content. This means that while the visible watermark may be gone, the underlying technology to detect AI-generated content remains in place.
