# OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Desktop App for Linux
OpenAI has launched a dedicated ChatGPT desktop app for Linux. This app allows users to interact with ChatGPT directly from their desktop. The move is significant as it indicates OpenAI's commitment to expanding its reach across various operating systems. By providing a native app for Linux, OpenAI aims to enhance the user experience and make its AI-powered chatbot more accessible to a broader audience.

## Onit: A Source-Available Alternative for Mac
Onit is a source-available ChatGPT desktop app with local mode, Claude, and Gemini support, designed for Mac. It does not contain code for Onit Dictate or QuickEdit. Onit SideKick is an AI chat sidebar that docks to any Mac app, allowing users to highlight text and open Onit with the selected text ready to use. This feature is particularly useful for users who want to integrate AI-powered tools into their workflow seamlessly. Onit's focus on Mac integration, rather than Linux compatibility, highlights the diversity of solutions emerging in the AI-powered tool landscape.
