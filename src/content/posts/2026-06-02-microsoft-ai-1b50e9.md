## Microsoft Expands AI Capabilities
Microsoft on Tuesday took the wraps off Adaptive Spec-driven Scoring for Evaluation and Regression Testing, an open source framework for spinning up AI evaluations. The specification lets developer, compliance, and security teams define their own policies for agents to follow in portable policy files.

The company also launched Scout, an OpenClaw-inspired personal assistant meant to bring the power and flexibility of OpenClaw into the Microsoft 365 system. Launched at Build, Microsoft Scout is a new AI assistant that automates dull office tasks.

## History of Microsoft AI Development
Microsoft has been working on various conversational AI projects over the last few years. The company launched its Tay.ai bot and continues to work on other AI bots, including Xiaoice, Ruuh, Rinna, and Zo.ai. Microsoft has been promoting the full-duplex chat capabilities of Xiaoice this year.

Microsoft officials believe that natural language will become the new user interface at some point. The company has been acquiring AI development companies, including conversational AI-focused Semantic Machines, reinforcement learning and simulation vendor Bonsai, and deep learning and AI model specialist Lobe.

## Microsoft and Nvidia Accelerate AI Development
Together, Microsoft and NVIDIA are accelerating some of the most groundbreaking innovations in AI. This long-standing collaboration has been at the core of the AI revolution over the past few years. The companies are integrating the newest NVIDIA Blackwell platform with Azure AI services infrastructure and incorporating NVIDIA NIM microservices into Azure AI Foundry.
