## AI in the Workplace
GitLab has cut 14% of its staff as it scales its platform to serve AI workloads. The company is reducing its workforce and exiting 22 countries, while also reducing management layers and investing in its infrastructure. 
This move is part of a broader trend of companies adapting to the growing use of AI in the workplace. As AI becomes more prevalent, companies are having to rethink their strategies and invest in new technologies.

## AI Regulation
Regulators are starting to take notice of the growing use of AI, with the U.K. requiring Google to offer a tool allowing website publishers to opt-out of generative AI search features. 
This move is seen as a significant step towards regulating the use of AI, and could have implications for the wider tech industry. As AI becomes more integrated into our lives, there will be a growing need for regulation and oversight.

## AI in Daily Life
