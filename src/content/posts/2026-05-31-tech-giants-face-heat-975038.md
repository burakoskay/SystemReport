## The Call for More Competition
An independent panel of experts says UK competition rules must be updated to address the issue of tech giants' dominance. The panel, led by Harvard Professor Jason Furman, recommends setting up a new competition unit and strengthening outdated laws to increase competition in the digital sector. The goal is to create a more level playing field, allowing smaller businesses to innovate and compete.

The panel's recommendations aim to give people more control over their data, provide small businesses with a better chance to enter and thrive, and create more predictability for large digital companies. The UK government has been urged to take action to address the issue of tech giants' dominance, which is limiting competition and consumer choice.
