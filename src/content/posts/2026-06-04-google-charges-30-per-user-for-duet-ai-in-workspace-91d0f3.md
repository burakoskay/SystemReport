## Pricing launch and immediate impact
Google announced that large enterprises will pay $30 per month for each Duet AI user in Google Workspace. The charge sits on top of existing Workspace subscriptions. This move is part of Google's effort to integrate generative AI into its core productivity suites, following the release of OpenAI's ChatGPT chatbot late last year. The pricing is aggressive, as it matches Microsoft's comparable 365 Copilot offering, which is set to be available in the first half of next year.

## New Gemini search in Drive and expanded AI tools
Google integrated Gemini into Drive, allowing users to ask the model to locate emails stored in Gmail. The feature works by typing a natural-language query in Drive's search bar. Gemini then rummages through linked Gmail accounts and returns matching messages. Duet AI also powers prompts in Gmail and general productivity apps. In Gmail, users type a brief request and receive generated text. For instance, users can ask Duet AI to draft an email based on a prompt, and the AI will generate the content.
