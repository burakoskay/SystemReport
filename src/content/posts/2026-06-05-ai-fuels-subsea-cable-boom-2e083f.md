## History of Subsea Cables

The first commercial telecommunication subsea cable was laid across the English Channel in 1850. Since then, the technology has evolved significantly, from coaxial cables to fiber optics. The development of subsea cables has been marked by significant milestones, including the first transatlantic cable in 1956 and the first fiber optic cable in 1988. Today, subsea cables carry over 95% of international data and voice call traffic, and their importance will only continue to grow.

## Technical Mechanics

Subsea cables are complex systems that require careful planning and execution. They consist of multiple layers, including the fiber optic core, the strength member, and the outer sheath. The fiber optic core is the most critical component, as it carries the data signals. The strength member provides additional support and protection, while the outer sheath protects the cable from environmental factors. The development of subsea cables requires significant expertise and investment, but the rewards are substantial.
