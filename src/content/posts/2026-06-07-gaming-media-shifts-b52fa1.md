## The Impact of Layoffs on Polygon

The sudden job cuts at Polygon appear to have been completely unexpected for those affected, with co-founder and editor-in-chief Chris Plante and Senior Writer Michael McWhertor among those let go. The move comes alongside the sale of Polygon to Internet brand aggregator Valnet, which has raised concerns about the future of the publication.

Valnet publishes dozens of Internet content brands, which collectively have over 260 million page views. However, the company has faced criticism for its treatment of writers, with some claiming they receive low pay for long articles. Despite this, Valnet insists that working conditions are good, and it has even sued The Wrap in federal court over allegations of poor treatment of writers.

## A 30-Year-Old Gaming News Site Perseveres

In contrast to the turmoil at Polygon, Blue's News, a gaming news site created in 1996, has persevered for over 30 years. The site was founded by Stephen "Blue" Heaslip as a Quake blog and has since become a general PC gaming and tech outlet with minimal changes since its inception.

Heaslip's commitment to providing concise, neutral news roundups and aggregated headlines has allowed the site to maintain its integrity, even as the media landscape around it has changed significantly. The site's social media tagline, "No AI slop, no sponsored posts. Sorry, not sorry," reflects its dedication to providing quality content without sensationalist headlines or sponsored posts.
