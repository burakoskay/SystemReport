Apple cut the list price of its third‑generation AirPods Pro to $179, while the M6 Mac Mini now sells for $779. The moves arrive as retailers roll out early Black Friday deals for 2026.

9to5Mac reports the AirPods Pro 3 normally cost $249, a $70 discount that puts them at the lowest price point ever recorded. The same outlet notes the Mac Mini sale starts at $779, a price the site calls the best for a mini computer. Both discounts appear before the official Black Friday weekend, suggesting retailers are trying to lock in early traffic.

## Apple’s AirPods Pro 3 price cut

The AirPods Pro 3 price cut is the most aggressive discount Apple has offered on its flagship earbuds since the Pro line launched. At $179, the set undercuts many premium true‑wireless competitors that still sit near $200. The reduction also aligns the product with the price of previous‑generation AirPods, potentially nudging owners of older models toward an upgrade.

The discount arrives amid a broader Apple pricing shuffle that includes the Apple Watch Series 12 and AirTag 2 hitting historic lows, according to the same 9to5Mac story. The simultaneous drops hint at a coordinated effort to clear inventory before newer hardware arrives later in the year.

## M6 Mac Mini hits $779 in early Black Friday sale

WIRED’s coverage of the M6 Mac Mini sale describes the machine as "the best mini computer you can buy" and highlights the $779 price tag as a standout for the category. The Mini packs Apple’s M6 silicon, which delivers a performance uplift over the previous generation, though the article does not list exact benchmarks.

At $779 the Mac Mini undercuts comparable Windows‑based mini PCs that often start above $900 for similar CPU and GPU configurations. The price may force small‑business buyers and hobbyists to reconsider the Mac ecosystem, especially given the Mac Mini’s reputation for low power draw and compact footprint.

## Parallel moves in peripherals and software

WIRED’s review of the Logitech Yeti 2 notes that the follow‑up to the iconic Yeti microphone introduces upgrades that "make it worthy of the legacy." While the review does not disclose a price, the implication is that the Yeti 2 improves on its predecessor’s sound quality and build without abandoning the brand’s established market position.

On the software side, 9to5Google confirms that Google has extended the Pixel 11 Pro’s Hilight feature to work with WhatsApp messages. The feature, which was bare‑bones at launch, now highlights text in a way that mirrors the native Android messaging experience. The addition broadens the utility of Hilight for users who rely on WhatsApp for daily communication.

## Context: pricing pressure in 2026 consumer tech

The early discounts reflect a broader pricing pressure that has built up over the past year. Semiconductor supply constraints eased in early 2026, allowing manufacturers to lower margins without sacrificing inventory levels. Retailers, in turn, have leveraged the easing to launch promotions ahead of the traditional holiday surge.

Apple’s aggressive pricing also responds to competition from Android OEMs that have been offering high‑spec earbuds and compact PCs at comparable price points. The iPhone Duo foldable, recently announced, adds another variable to the market, but its pricing remains undisclosed, leaving the AirPods and Mac Mini as the most concrete price signals for now.

## What to watch

Track the official Black Friday weekend pricing for the AirPods Pro 3 and the M6 Mac Mini; retailers often deepen discounts as the holiday rush peaks. Also watch for any follow‑up announcements from Logitech on Yeti 2 pricing and from Google on further Hilight integrations, especially with other third‑party messaging apps. Those data points will clarify whether the early cuts are isolated promotions or part of a larger shift in premium‑hardware pricing strategy.
