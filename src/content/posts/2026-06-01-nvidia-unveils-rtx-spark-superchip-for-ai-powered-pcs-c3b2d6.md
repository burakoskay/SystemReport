## Nvidia and Microsoft Unveil RTX Spark Superchip for AI-Powered Windows PCs

Nvidia unveiled the NVIDIA RTX Spark™ superchip at GTC Taipei, a new superchip that reinvents Windows PCs for the era of personal AI agents — offering a new class of computer that moves from tool to teammate. This superchip is designed to bring AI agents to Windows PCs, enabling users to interact with their computers in a more natural and intuitive way.

The RTX Spark superchip brings together 30 years of Nvidia innovation, including CUDA®, RTX™, DLSS, FP4, TensorRT™, OptiX™, Reflex, and G-SYNC®. This powerful combination enables on-device agents that can execute tasks in Windows applications, reason through cross-app workflows, generate images and video, code plug-ins and apps, and semantically search local files. For instance, users can ask their AI agent to create a presentation using data from various applications, or to generate a video based on a set of images.
