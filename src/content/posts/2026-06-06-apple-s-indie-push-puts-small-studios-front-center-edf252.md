# Apple's Indie Push Puts Small Studios Front-Center

Apple has introduced a permanent Indie Game Showcase on the App Store's home screen, highlighting independent studios and their titles. The showcase features Sweden's Simogo and its interactive-fiction puzzle title Device 6. Apple also rolled out a "Games" category, which includes a list of essential indie games.

## Device 6: Interactive-Fiction Puzzle Title

Device 6 blends text-driven narrative with tactile puzzles, requiring players to swipe, tap, and rotate the device to progress. The experience is built for iPhone, iPad, and iPod touch. Apple highlighted Device 6 as a showcase title, citing its nominations for the Innovation Award and Best Handheld/Mobile Game at the Game Developers Choice Awards.

## Supermash: Genre-Bending Indie Title

Supermash, a genre-bending indie title, has landed on Switch in May 2020. The game allows players to combine platforming with stealth, or shoot-'em-up action with JRPG battles. The game's core loop asks players to pick two genres, then watch the engine splice their mechanics together in real time.

## The Broader Indie Ecosystem on Mobile and Console

Apple reported that more than $10 billion was spent on the App Store last year. The majority of that spend still funnels into a handful of blockbuster titles, leaving a thin slice for smaller creators. By carving out a permanent showcase, Apple is trying to shift a few percentage points of that spend toward indie projects.

## Industry Context and History

The indie surge began in the early 2010s when digital storefronts lowered distribution costs. Apple's App Store, launched in 2008, quickly became the primary gateway for mobile games. Simogo's Device 6 rode that wave, earning awards that validated mobile-first design. Supermash follows a lineage of genre-mixing experiments, from Nier Automata's blend of hack-and-slash and bullet-hell to Crypt of the NecroDancer's rhythm-based roguelike.

## History of Apple's Indie-Focused Initiatives

In 2010, Apple launched the App Store's "New and Noteworthy" section, which featured innovative and critically acclaimed titles. Simogo's Device 6 was one of the first games to benefit from this section, earning widespread recognition and critical acclaim. This success paved the way for future indie-focused initiatives, including the permanent Indie Game Showcase.

## The Technical Mechanics of Indie Game Development

Indie game development often involves a small team of developers working together to create a cohesive and engaging experience. In the case of Supermash, the game's core mechanic relies on a complex algorithm that combines the elements of two genres, creating a unique experience each time. This mechanic requires a deep understanding of game development and programming, making it a challenging and rewarding experience for indie developers.

## Downstream Implications of Apple's Indie Push

Apple's permanent indie banner and Nintendo's genre-mash experiments illustrate a growing belief that indie innovation can sustain platform relevance. The next rotation of Apple's showcase and the rollout of Nintendo's genre API will be litmus tests for whether these strategies translate into measurable market shifts.

## Industry Reaction and Future Outlook

The gaming industry has responded positively to Apple's indie-focused initiatives, with many developers expressing their gratitude for the added visibility and support. As the gaming landscape continues to evolve, it will be interesting to see how indie developers adapt and innovate, pushing the boundaries of what is possible in the industry.
