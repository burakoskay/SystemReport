## Introduction to Recent Tech Updates

Google Messages is rolling out a new 'Tap to draft' behavior for Smart Replies, aiming to prevent accidental sends. This feature, tested in March, is now available to all users. In other news, Instagram has introduced a significant change to its feed, switching from the traditional up-and-down scrolling to a side-to-side format, similar to its Stories feature.

## Google Messages Update

The 'Tap to draft' feature is a direct response to user frustration with accidental sends. By requiring users to tap to draft a response, Google Messages reduces the likelihood of sending unintended replies. This update is part of Google's ongoing effort to improve the messaging experience. For instance, the company has also redesigned Google Maps alerts on Android Auto to be less distracting, enhancing the overall user experience. The redesign of Google Maps alerts is a significant improvement, as it reduces the risk of accidents caused by distracted driving.

## Instagram's Feed Update

Instagram's decision to change its feed scrolling has been met with widespread outrage from users. The update, which was rolled out by mistake according to Instagram's boss, changes the way users interact with the app, making it more similar to the Stories feature. Users have expressed frustration, with many calling for the return of the traditional timeline. The update's intention was to mimic the success of Instagram Stories, which has become a fast-growing media format, but it seems to have backfired. Instagram's move has sparked a heated debate among users, with some arguing that the new format is more engaging, while others claim it is more confusing.

## WhatsApp and Snapchat Updates

WhatsApp is testing disappearing messages, allowing users to set a time limit after which the messages will self-destruct. This feature, available in the beta version for Android, aims to enhance privacy. Snapchat, on the other hand, may introduce ads that users can only skip after three seconds, a move that could potentially frustrate users but generate more revenue for the company. The introduction of disappearing messages on WhatsApp is a significant step towards enhancing user privacy, as it provides an additional layer of security for sensitive conversations.

## Apple Watch and Other Tech News

The original Apple Watch Edition, which was once the most expensive consumer product by Apple, is now obsolete after the latest watchOS update. The watch, made from rare metals, was sold for over $10,000 but is no longer compatible with the latest software. In other news, Microsoft's Windows update has caused a web blackout, and there are reports of a potential new ad format for Snapchat that would force users to watch ads for at least three seconds before being able to skip them. The Apple Watch Edition's obsolescence is a reminder of the rapid pace of technological advancements, which can quickly render even the most expensive and exclusive products obsolete.
