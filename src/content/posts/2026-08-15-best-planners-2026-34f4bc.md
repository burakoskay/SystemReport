## Introduction to Paper Planners
Roterunner, Hobonichi, and Cloth & Paper are WIRED's top planners of 2026. These paper agendas and notebooks offer a tangible alternative to digital calendars, allowing users to manage their time and tasks in a more traditional and tactile way.

## The Benefits of Paper Planners
Paper planners provide a clear overview of the user's schedule, helping them to prioritize tasks, manage time more effectively, and reduce stress. Hobonichi's planners are known for their high-quality paper and binding, providing a premium feel that makes them a joy to use. Roterunner offers a minimalistic design that focuses on essential tasks and appointments, allowing users to cut through the clutter and stay focused on what's important.

## History of Paper Planners
The use of paper planners dates back to the early 20th century, when they were first used by businesspeople and professionals to manage their schedules and appointments. Over the years, their design and functionality have evolved to meet the changing needs of users, with the introduction of new features such as to-do lists, goal-setting templates, and inspirational quotes. Today, paper planners are more than just a scheduling tool - they are a personal statement and a reflection of one's personality, values, and style.
