## Real-Time Music Sharing
Snapchat users can share their Spotify listening activity in real time. Users link their Spotify accounts, choose who can see their activity, and start sharing. This feature allows for a new level of social interaction, enabling friends to discover new music and discuss their listening habits.

## Music Streaming Integration
Snapchat allows users to link their Spotify accounts and share listening activity. Users have control over who can see their activity, giving them control over their online presence. The integration of Spotify into Snapchat expands the social media platform's capabilities, providing a more engaging experience for users.

## Technical Mechanics
The technical mechanics behind the integration of Spotify into Snapchat involve the use of APIs and data sharing agreements. Spotify's API allows Snapchat to access user listening activity, which is then shared on the social media platform. The data sharing agreement between the two companies ensures that user data is protected and shared in accordance with privacy laws. This agreement is crucial in maintaining user trust and ensuring that the integration is successful.
