# Apple Shakes Up AI Team Ahead of Siri Upgrades

Apple's AI efforts have been lackluster, but the company is trying to change that. In a move to boost its artificial intelligence capabilities, Apple is making Kim Vorrath, who helped get the Vision Pro software out the door, the top deputy to AI boss John Giannandrea.

Vorrath's 36-year tenure at Apple began with the original iPhone software group. Her move to the AI team is a clear indication that Apple sees AI as a key component of its future. The company made a big splash about its AI efforts at WWDC last year, but it hasn't had the same impact as OpenAI's ChatGPT or Google's Gemini.

## Apple's AI Infrastructure Revamp

The company's AI team is focused on revamping Siri's underlying infrastructure and improving in-house AI models this year. This move suggests that Apple is taking a more rigorous approach to AI development. The Vision Pro, a product that Apple hyped, seems to be taking a backseat to AI.

## Siri Upgrade Delayed

Apple's big Siri upgrade, which lets it understand what's happening on your screen and take action, may not arrive until iOS 18.4. The company's AI-powered news notification summaries will also be put on pause with iOS 18.3, following criticism that the summaries were incorrect.
