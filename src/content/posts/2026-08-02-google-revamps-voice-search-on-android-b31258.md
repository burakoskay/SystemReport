## Introduction
Google's voice search button on Android now merges AI Mode, Search Live, and Song lookup into one interface.
The redesign affects the voice search button on the Google bar found on most Android homescreens, combining features for a unified experience.

## Background
The voice search button has been a mainstay on Android homescreens, allowing quick access to information and tasks via voice commands. This integration underscores Google's efforts to streamline user interaction, making it easier for users to find what they need without navigating through multiple features.

## Technical Details
The merged AI Mode, Search Live, and Song lookup features are now accessible through a single interface, simplifying the voice search experience. This change indicates a shift towards more intuitive design, focusing on user convenience and reducing the complexity associated with navigating multiple, separate features.
