## $500 Million for Orbital Maneuvering

This investment comes at a critical juncture as the orbital maneuvering market experiences significant growth. The funds will be used to expand the company's team and enhance its capabilities in developing advanced propulsion systems. Specifically, Romo emphasized that the capital will be allocated towards recruiting top talent in engineering and manufacturing.
