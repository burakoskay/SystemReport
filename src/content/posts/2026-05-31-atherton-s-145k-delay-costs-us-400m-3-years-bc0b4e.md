## Atherton's $145K Decision Delayed Train Electrification
According to a Peninsula for Everyone blog post, Atherton spent $145,000 to delay the electrification of Caltrain, a commuter rail system connecting San Francisco to the peninsula. The delay has cost the rest of the state $400 million and added three extra years to the project timeline.

## The Cost of Delay
The city's decision was driven by a desire to mitigate the financial impact of the project, but the move ultimately resulted in increased costs for materials, labor, and maintenance. The California Public Utilities Commission has taken a closer look at the project's timeline and budget, resulting in calls for greater transparency and accountability.

## The History of Caltrain Electrification
Caltrain's electrification project has been a long-standing goal, with initial plans dating back to 2008. Despite setbacks and local opposition, the project has continued to push forward, albeit at a slower pace. The project's timeline has been repeatedly delayed, with some communities expressing concerns over the project's impact on local businesses.

## The Broader Industry Context
The Caltrain electrification project is part of a larger effort to modernize the state's transportation infrastructure. Other rail systems, such as the BART and SF MUNI, are also undergoing electrification projects. The market for rail electrification is growing, with companies like Siemens and Bombardier providing equipment and services for these projects.

## The Technical Mechanics
The electrification of Caltrain involves the installation of new electrical infrastructure, including power substations and catenary systems. The project also requires the replacement of diesel locomotives with electric trains. The technical complexity of the project has contributed to delays and cost overruns.
