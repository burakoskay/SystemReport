## Poland's Phone Ban

The Polish government has approved a bill to ban phones and smartwatches in schools, aiming to reduce distractions and improve learning. The ban, which is expected to take effect soon, will prohibit students from using phones and smartwatches during lessons, with some exceptions for students with disabilities or medical conditions. According to the bill, teachers and school administrators will be responsible for enforcing the ban.
