# CodeCrafters launches public challenges on Hacker News

CodeCrafters, a Y Combinator S22 alumni, opened its first public challenges on Hacker News, promising a hands-on approach for engineers to rebuild real systems from scratch. The platform lists 11 challenges covering TCP servers, the HTTP protocol, Redis, Git, SQLite, and more. Developers at the world's best companies enjoy CodeCrafters, which teaches skills like executing programs, parsing shell commands, and implementing core components of production software.

## CodeCrafters' hands-on approach

CodeCrafters positions itself as a replacement for algorithm-drill sites, focusing on real-world projects. The platform supplies a test harness that runs against a hidden reference implementation, providing pass/fail results and contextual hints after a git push. This approach allows engineers to learn by doing, rather than just reading or watching tutorials. For instance, CodeCrafters' challenges encourage developers to rebuild systems like Redis, Git, and SQLite from scratch, in their preferred language and IDE.

## ntsc-rs brings analog TV artifacts to the web

The open-source project ntsc-rs appeared on Hacker News, garnering 202 points and 34 comments. The repository provides a Rust implementation of video emulation of analog TV and VHS artifacts, which can be dropped into media pipelines. Developers can add authentic analog grain to retro-style games, video editors, or archival tools. This project demonstrates the growing interest in preserving and emulating classic media formats.

## Mathpad's hardware shortcut for math symbols

A third thread on Hacker News showcased Mathpad, a hardware keypad designed to type mathematical symbols. The limited-time '24 hours left' Show HN post includes a short demonstration of the keypad's ability to produce Greek letters, integrals, and over 100 other symbols with a single tap. Mathpad aims to eliminate the friction of typing complex mathematical expressions, making it easier for engineers, teachers, and software developers to document calculations and create worksheets.
