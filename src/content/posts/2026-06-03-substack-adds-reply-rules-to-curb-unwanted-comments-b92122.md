Substack's new Reply Rules feature is now available for all English-language publications. It lets writers set parameters that determine which replies appear publicly.

## A new way to manage comment sections

The feature gives creators greater control over how their audiences respond. This level of control is crucial for independent writers who rely on comment sections to gauge reader sentiment and spark ideas for future pieces. When the signal gets drowned by noise, the value of the interaction drops dramatically.

## The need for moderation tools

Without effective moderation tools, comment sections can quickly become overwhelmed by spam, harassment, or off-topic discussions. This not only detracts from the reader experience but also discourages writers from engaging with their audience. By introducing Reply Rules, Substack aims to empower creators to maintain a high-quality discussion around their content.

## How Reply Rules work

Reply Rules allow creators to set specific parameters for their comment sections. For example, they can choose to automatically approve comments from subscribers, or manually review and approve comments before they go live. This level of control enables creators to tailor their moderation approach to their specific needs and audience.
