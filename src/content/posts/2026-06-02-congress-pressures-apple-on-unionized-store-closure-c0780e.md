## Congressional Pushback

Dozens of members of Congress are urging Apple to reconsider its plan to close the Towson Town Center store in Maryland, the first Apple Store in the US to unionize. The lawmakers accuse Apple of potentially retaliating against workers.

The store, located in Towson, Maryland, unionized in June 2022, with employees voting to join the International Association of Machinists and Aerospace Workers. Apple announced plans to close the store. 

In a letter to Apple, the lawmakers expressed concern that the closure could be a form of retaliation against workers for organizing. 

The lawmakers' concerns are not unfounded. The National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) has been actively involved in addressing labor disputes at Apple Stores. In 2022, the NLRB filed a complaint against Apple, alleging unfair labor practices.
