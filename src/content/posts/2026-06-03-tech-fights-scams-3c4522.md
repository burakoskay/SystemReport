## Google Takes on Deepfake Scams
Google has introduced a new feature to detect and prevent AI deepfake impersonation scams. These scams involve spoofing trusted phone numbers and using AI technology to sound like authority figures, family members, or employers. According to TechCrunch, scammers are shifting their tactics as people increasingly refuse to answer calls from unknown numbers.

The new feature aims to protect users from these types of scams by detecting and flagging suspicious calls. This move is significant, given the rise of deepfake technology and its potential for misuse. Google's effort to combat these scams is a crucial step in ensuring user safety and security.

## Meta's Tracking Program
Meta is reportedly allowing employees to take 30-minute breaks from its tracking program. This move is seen as a response to employee concerns about the all-seeing eye of the company's tracking system. As reported by Engadget, workers can pause the tracking program when they need to check something personal.

This development raises questions about the balance between employee privacy and company tracking. While Meta's tracking program is intended to monitor employee activity, it is essential to consider the potential impact on employee well-being and productivity. The ability to take breaks from the tracking program may help alleviate some of these concerns.
