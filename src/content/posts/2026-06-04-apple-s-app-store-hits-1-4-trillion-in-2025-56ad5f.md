## Introduction to App Store's Success
Apple's App Store generated $1.4 trillion in sales in 2025, up from $1.3 trillion in 2024. $149 billion of the total were digital-goods sales. Over 90% of the billings did not involve a commission to Apple.

## Scale and Commission-Free Narrative
Apple frames the 90% commission-free rate as a key statistic. The study shows that out of $1.4 trillion in global billings, roughly $1.26 trillion flowed through transactions that Apple did not tax.

## Where the Money Comes From
Digital-goods sales were $149 billion in 2025. The study was conducted by economists at Analysis Group. Mobile games, photo and video editing apps, and enterprise tools drive this demand.

## Developers and the App Store
Apple's App Store facilitated over $406 billion in developer billings and sales in the US in 2024. Earnings of US developers more than doubled in the last five years. This growth is significant and indicates the importance of the App Store for developers.

## Regulatory Pressure
A 2021 court ruling forced Apple to let developers link to external payment pages. The Digital Markets Act obliges Apple to disclose alternative payment options to users. This regulatory pressure has significant implications for Apple and the App Store.
