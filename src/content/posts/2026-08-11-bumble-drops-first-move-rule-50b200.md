## Introduction to the Change
Bumble has ditched its rule that kept men from making the first move, a policy it has enforced since its launch in 2024. This change means that either person in a match can now send the first message.
## Background on the Policy Change
The decision to change the messaging policy was first teased by the company in May. Bumble was previously unique among major dating apps for enforcing a first-move rule. The company's move to change this policy could be seen as a way to increase user engagement and make the app more appealing to a wider range of users.
## Impact on Users
Bumble's update allows either person to send the first message after matching. The company had enforced its previous rule since 2024. This change is likely to have a significant impact on user behavior, as it gives both parties in a match the ability to initiate a conversation.
## Industry Context
