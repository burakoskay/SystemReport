## Technical Mechanics of Portable Solar Panels
Portable solar panels work on the principle of photovoltaics, where sunlight is converted into electrical energy. The panels consist of multiple solar cells made from semiconducting materials like silicon.

The current is then collected and stored in a battery or used to charge devices directly. The quality of the solar cells, the surface area of the panel, and the amount of sunlight it receives all impact the panel's efficiency.

## History and Development
The development of portable solar panels has come a long way since the first solar cells were invented in the 1950s. Over the years, significant advancements have been made in the design and manufacture of solar panels, leading to more efficient and affordable products.

One of the key milestones in the development of portable solar panels was the introduction of monocrystalline silicon solar cells, which offer higher efficiency and durability compared to earlier technologies. More recently, the development of bifacial solar cells has further improved the efficiency of solar panels, allowing them to generate electricity from both the front and back sides.
