## The Dell XPS 13 Re-launch
Dell is making good on its tease from CES and announcing a new XPS 13. The laptop launches in July at a promotional student price of $599, a deal that only runs until September for back-to-school shopping; it will start at $699 for everyone else.
## Pricing and Student Targeting
Dell's promotional price matches Apple's student price for the Neo. The limited-time discount is aimed at students. Dell's price will increase to $699 after the promotional period.
## Design and Performance
The new XPS 13 is a budget-friendly option. Dell's decision to offer the XPS 13 at a lower price point may impact its performance. The company's goal is to provide an affordable laptop for students.
