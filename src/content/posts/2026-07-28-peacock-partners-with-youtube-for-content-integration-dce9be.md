## Introduction to the Deal
Peacock has announced a deal with YouTube to integrate its content into the YouTube experience. This allows viewers to discover and watch Peacock content without leaving YouTube.
According to the terms, Peacock content will be fully incorporated into YouTube's interface, making it easy for users to find and watch their favorite shows.

## The Strategy Behind the Deal
Peacock's decision to partner with YouTube reflects a strategic choice to prioritize accessibility and convenience for its viewers. By integrating its content into YouTube, Peacock aims to increase its visibility and attract new viewers.
The deal benefits Peacock by reaching YouTube's vast user base, presenting an opportunity to promote its content to viewers who may not have been aware of its offerings. This approach enables Peacock to capitalize on YouTube's extensive reach and user engagement.
