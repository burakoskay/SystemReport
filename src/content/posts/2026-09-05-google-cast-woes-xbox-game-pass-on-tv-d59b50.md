## Indie games that bite back

A cat in a pixel‑perfect office fights an AI takeover in the new indie title *Cat vs. AI*. The game appears in a roundup that also flags *Remote Work as a Worm* and a ship‑building sandbox.[^1][^2] The list arrives from Engadget’s weekly indie roundup.[^1][^2]

The article bundles fifteen titles, from platformers that splash color to narrative experiments that riff on remote‑work fatigue.[^1][^3] It notes an early look at the *I Am Your Beast* DLC, which adds a cooperative mode to the horror‑action hybrid.[^1][^2][^4][^5] The roundup gives no pricing, but it supplies enough detail for hobbyists to add the games to their backlog.

## Disney+ stripped of Google Cast in several markets

Disney+ stopped working with Google Cast in a handful of countries, according to 9to5Google.[^6][^7][^8] The rollback stems from a patent dispute that has already knocked the feature out of the app’s European builds.[^6]

The loss affects users who rely on Chromecast or built‑in Cast on Android TV to stream Disney+ to larger screens. The article does not list the affected nations, but it confirms the issue is not a temporary glitch; the app’s code now omits the Cast flag in the regions where the dispute is active.[^6]

Industry analysts note that Disney’s broader licensing strategy already leans heavily on its own hardware and partner agreements. The Cast removal could push more subscribers toward Roku or Amazon Fire TV, where Disney+ still casts without friction.

## Xbox Game Pass finally lands a Google TV client

Microsoft acknowledged a Google TV client for Xbox Game Pass, targeting TCL smart‑TVs, 9to5Google reports. The app will debut in the “coming months,” a timeline that matches a teaser posted nearly a year ago.

The client will let subscribers launch Game Pass titles directly from the TV interface, bypassing the need for a console or Android box. Microsoft’s statement frames the rollout as a “TCL‑first” effort, implying a broader Google TV rollout could follow.

The move signals Microsoft’s intent to capture living‑room gamers who favor streaming over traditional hardware. It also positions Game Pass against rival subscription services that already run on Google TV, such as Netflix and Disney+.

## Samsung restocks the Fold 8, trims Ultra price

Samsung has fully restocked its Galaxy Z Fold 8, according to 9to5Google. The restock arrives as trade‑in volumes tumble, prompting the company to replenish inventory that had been throttled by supply constraints.

At the same time, Samsung cut the price of the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra to $1,799. The price drop narrows the gap between the standard Fold and its premium sibling, which previously commanded a higher premium.

The pricing shift could revive demand among power users who balked at the Ultra’s earlier tag. Analysts see the move as a response to a softening premium‑fold market and a bid to keep Samsung’s foldable lineup competitive against rivals like the Google Pixel Fold.

## What this means for the ecosystem

The three developments illustrate a broader tension between platform openness and proprietary control. Disney+ losing Cast support shows how patent disputes can force streaming services to retreat from cross‑device features. Xbox Game Pass’s Google TV app demonstrates Microsoft’s willingness to embed its service in a competitor’s ecosystem, but only after a lengthy tease. Samsung’s restock and price cut reveal how hardware manufacturers must balance supply chain volatility with pricing pressure.

For developers, the indie game roundup underscores that discovery still relies on editorial curation rather than algorithmic recommendation. For consumers, the Cast rollback and Game Pass expansion highlight a fragmented streaming landscape where feature parity depends on legal and strategic decisions more than technical capability.

## What to watch next

Track Disney+’s legal filings for any settlement that could restore Cast in the affected regions. Keep an eye on Microsoft’s rollout schedule; a broader Google TV launch would signal a shift in how Microsoft approaches living‑room gaming. Monitor Samsung’s inventory levels and pricing through the next quarter to see if the Fold 8 Ultra’s discount stabilizes demand or triggers a deeper price war in the foldable segment.
