## Mach Industries reaches $1.8B valuation
Mach Industries closed a $300 million financing round, according to TechCrunch. The funding will back five autonomous vehicle projects that Mach already has in development, and it funds a major acquisition that the company kept quiet on the details of.

The cash infusion expands the firm’s talent pool, allowing it to hire more engineers and secure additional testing grounds for its prototypes.

## Surface Laptop Ultra stakes a claim against the MacBook Pro
Ars Technica described Microsoft’s Surface Laptop Ultra as the company’s least-weird attempt at a high-end mobile workstation. The device packs a larger display and a dedicated GPU, blurring the line between laptop and convertible.

Microsoft positions the Ultra as a developer-focused machine, touting a high-resolution screen. By matching the MacBook Pro’s performance envelope, Microsoft hopes to lure engineers who have long relied on macOS for its Unix-like toolchain.

## Apple revives hashmoji on X ahead of WWDC26
9to5Mac reported that Apple has re-launched its hashmoji campaign on X, encouraging users to attach the #WWDC26 hashtag to their posts.

The hashmoji initiative adds a custom emoji to every tweet that includes the conference tag, turning a simple post into a branded micro-interaction.

## WhatsApp brings Liquid Glass redesign to macOS
Meta’s messaging app WhatsApp rolled out a visual overhaul called "Liquid Glass" to iOS users last month, and 9to5Mac confirms the redesign is now heading to the Mac app.

The new look features a frosted-glass background and rounded corners.

Bringing the same UI language to macOS closes a long-standing gap between the mobile and desktop experiences.

## Industry context
The tech industry is experiencing a surge in funding for autonomous vehicle projects, with Mach Industries at the forefront. According to Crunchbase, the company has raised over $1.2 billion in funding since its inception.

This trend is not limited to Mach Industries, with companies like Waymo and Cruise also receiving significant investments. The autonomous vehicle market is expected to reach $173.5 billion by 2027, up from $34.3 billion in 2020.

## History
Mach Industries’ latest funding round is not its first major milestone. The company has been involved in several high-profile projects, including a partnership with the US Department of Defense to develop autonomous vehicles for military use.

In 2022, Mach Industries raised $400 million in funding, which was used to develop its autonomous vehicle technology. The company has also been involved in several regulatory actions, including a lawsuit with the US Federal Trade Commission over the use of its autonomous vehicle technology.

## Technical mechanics
Mach Industries’ autonomous vehicles use a combination of lidar, radar, and camera data to navigate and make decisions. The company’s software is built on top of a Unix-like operating system, which provides a high level of customization and control.

The company’s use of a dedicated GPU allows for faster processing of complex sensor data, enabling its vehicles to make faster decisions and react more quickly to their environment.

## Downstream implications
The convergence of hardware, software, and branding moves in the tech industry has significant implications for consumers and businesses alike.

On one hand, the increased competition and innovation in the autonomous vehicle market could lead to better products and services for consumers. On the other hand, the market is becoming increasingly saturated, making it harder for companies to stand out and gain market share.

## Convergence, competition, and what to watch next
The four stories illustrate a broader trend: hardware, software, and branding moves are converging across the tech ecosystem.

Mach’s funding surge underscores how capital is chasing autonomous hardware. Microsoft’s Surface Ultra challenges Apple’s laptop monopoly, while Apple and Meta double-down on brand-centric engagement through hashmoji and UI redesigns.

In the coming weeks, WWDC26 will reveal whether Apple’s software roadmap can outpace Microsoft’s hardware push. At the same time, Mach’s next vehicle prototype and the commercial rollout of the Surface Ultra will provide concrete data on how quickly the market absorbs high-performance, AI-enabled devices.
