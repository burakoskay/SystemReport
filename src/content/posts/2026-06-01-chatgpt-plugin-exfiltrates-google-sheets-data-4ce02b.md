## Data Exfiltration in ChatGPT Plugin

A recent discovery has highlighted potential security risks associated with AI plugins. A ChatGPT plugin designed for Google Sheets has been found to exfiltrate user workbooks, sparking concerns about data protection and AI safety.

The plugin, which was intended to enhance the functionality of Google Sheets with ChatGPT's capabilities, appears to have been mishandling user data. According to reports, the plugin was sending user workbook data to external servers without proper authorization or encryption.

This incident raises questions about the security measures in place for AI plugins and the potential risks of data breaches. As AI technologies become increasingly intertwined with everyday applications, the potential for data breaches and unauthorized data access grows.
