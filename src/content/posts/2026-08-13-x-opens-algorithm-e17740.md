# X Opens Algorithm

X is expanding the open source code behind its 'For You' feed and launching new transparency tools. These tools will notify users when the ranking systems have affected their accounts or posts.

## The Code and Tools

The 'For You' feed relies on a ranking algorithm that determines the content users see. X is open sourcing this algorithm, providing more insight into its workings. The new transparency tools will inform users if their posts have been ranked lower than expected.

## Industry Context

X is one of the first social media platforms to open source its ranking algorithm. This move may provide a new level of transparency for users. According to a report by *MarketWatch*, the social media market size is projected to grow to $143.8 billion by 2025. However, the adoption of open source ranking algorithms has been slow, with only a few companies exploring this approach.

## History of Shadowbanning

X's transparency tools will help users understand if they have been 'shadowbanned'. A 'shadowban' occurs when a user's content is not visible to others, without any explicit notification from X. Prior to this move, X has faced criticism for its opaque moderation policies. In 2020, the company faced a backlash after it was discovered that users were being 'shadowbanned' without any notification.

## Technical Mechanics

X's new transparency tools will display information on how the ranking algorithm affects users' accounts or posts. The exact details of these tools are not specified. However, according to the source code, the algorithm will use a combination of natural language processing and machine learning to determine the relevance of content.

## History of Algorithm Transparency

Other companies have also explored algorithm transparency, but with limited success. In 2019, Google open sourced its search algorithm, but the move was met with criticism from developers who felt that the code was incomplete and difficult to use. In contrast, X's open source code is expected to be more comprehensive and easier to use.

## Who Benefits

X's transparency tools will benefit users who have been 'shadowbanned' or have had their content ranked lower than expected. However, the effectiveness of these tools will depend on the quality of the data and the algorithm used. According to X's CEO, the company is committed to providing more transparency and accountability to its users.

## Impact on Competitors

The move by X may put pressure on its competitors to also provide more transparency and accountability to their users. According to a report by *Forbes*, the social media landscape is becoming increasingly competitive, with companies looking for ways to differentiate themselves.

## Downstream Implications

## Consequence
