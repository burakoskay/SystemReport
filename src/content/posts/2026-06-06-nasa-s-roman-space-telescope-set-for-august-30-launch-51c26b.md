## The Telescope's Capabilities

The Roman Space Telescope boasts a 7.9-foot-wide primary mirror, similar in size to the Hubble Space Telescope's. However, its field of view is 100 times larger, allowing it to survey thousands of planetary bodies in the Milky Way and smaller celestial bodies in our solar system. This capability will enable scientists to address topics such as planetary science, stellar populations, galaxy evolution, and cosmology.

The telescope's design and technology will allow it to gather data that will help scientists better understand the mysteries surrounding dark energy, dark matter, and observe exoplanets. By combining the power of imaging and spectroscopy in synergy with other observatories, Roman will gain fresh insights into the universe through focused surveys and General Astrophysics observations.
