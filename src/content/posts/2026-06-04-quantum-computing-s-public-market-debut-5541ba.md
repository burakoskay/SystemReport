## Technical Mechanics
Quantum computing is based on the principles of quantum mechanics, which describe the behavior of particles at the atomic and subatomic level. Quantum computers use quantum bits or qubits, which can exist in multiple states simultaneously, allowing for the processing of vast amounts of information in parallel.

The technical mechanics of quantum computing are complex, involving the manipulation of qubits using quantum gates and other control mechanisms.
