## Introduction to AV2
The AV2 video standard is now version 1.0 and available on the Alliance for Open Media website. This development enables more efficient video streaming and storage. The AV2 standard results from collaborative efforts by industry leaders to create a more efficient video compression technology.
## The AV2 Video Standard
The AV2 standard compares favorably to its predecessor, AV1, and other codecs. Early implementation partners include major industry players. Licensing, patent pools, and hardware support for AV2 must be addressed. According to the Alliance for Open Media, the AV2 standard offers improved compression efficiency, which will lead to better video streaming quality and reduced bandwidth costs.
## Food Technologies and Land Use
New food technologies such as cellular agriculture and microbial protein production are being developed to address food production competing with biodiversity and carbon storage for land use. Cellular agriculture grows animal products from real animal cells, while microbial protein production uses bacteria to convert carbon dioxide and nitrogen into carbohydrates and proteins. These technologies can reduce the land required for food production. Regulatory hurdles must be overcome, and concerns about taste, texture, and nutritional content must be addressed.

## Technical Mechanics
The AV2 standard uses a range of technical innovations to achieve its improved compression efficiency. These include advanced techniques such as intra-block copying and adaptive quantization. The standard also supports a range of features such as HDR and 8K video, making it well-suited to a wide range of applications. The use of these technical innovations will enable the widespread adoption of high-quality video streaming, even in areas with limited internet connectivity.
