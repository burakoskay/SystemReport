## Layoffs Hit Restart
Restart, a gaming site sponsored by Walmart, laid off its entire editorial team on Friday, according to a post by Brandy Berthelson, the site's former editor-in-chief. Other staffers also confirmed being let go.

## Background on Restart
Restart, operated by Moonrock, a content marketing agency, launched in late 2024 with a unique model: it provided purchase links directing users to Walmart, using a partner tracking code. Brandy Berthelson stated, 'We were laid off due to financial issues.' The specifics of the partnership between Restart and Walmart are outlined on the site's editorial mission page, which states it is 'powered' by the retail giant.

The use of sponsored content in gaming media is not new, but it has become more prevalent in recent years. ## Technical Mechanics of Sponsored Content
The technical mechanics of sponsored content in gaming media involve the use of tracking codes and affiliate links. These links allow the site to earn a commission on sales generated through the link. In the case of Restart, the site used a partner tracking code to direct users to Walmart. This model can be effective, but it requires a high volume of traffic and sales to generate significant revenue. The site's inability to sustain this model ultimately led to the layoffs.
