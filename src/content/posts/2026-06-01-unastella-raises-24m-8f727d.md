## Unastella Rockets to New Heights with $24M Funding

Unastella, a Seoul-based rocket startup that originally launched rockets from a home garage, has secured $24 million in funding. The company is developing its own launch vehicles and engines. This investment comes as South Korea's interest in satellite technology and space exploration is on the rise, creating a supportive environment for Unastella's ambitions.

The funding round was likely influenced by the growing demand for satellite launches and the potential for private companies to play a significant role in this market. South Korea has been actively promoting its space industry, with initiatives such as the development of a domestic satellite launch vehicle.

South Korea's space industry has been growing rapidly over the past decade, with significant investments in satellite technology and launch vehicles.
