## AI and Secure Messaging

Secure messaging apps are supposed to keep the contents of every message confidential. However, introducing AI processing for messages brings new risks to the architecture that protects our privacy and security. Meta's LLMs don't run locally on your phone, so WhatsApp will have to send all your supposedly secure messages into Meta's servers so that the LLM can process them. This means that Meta's servers can read your messages, breaking their confidentiality. If you paste the contents of your secure messages into a network LLM service like ChatGPT, the operator of the service can read your messages.
