The Google Tasks widget on Android is finally getting an update with a Material 3 Expressive redesign. This change is part of Google's ongoing effort to revamp its apps and make them more visually appealing. The update is rolling out to users now.

The new design is more in line with Google's recent Material 3 updates for other apps, including Gboard. The Google Tasks widget now features a more modern and expressive look, making it easier to use and more aesthetically pleasing.

The Material 3 redesign for Google Tasks includes a refreshed layout and new visual elements. The update aims to provide a more cohesive and intuitive user experience. With this redesign, Google Tasks is now more consistent with other Google apps that have already received the Material 3 treatment.
