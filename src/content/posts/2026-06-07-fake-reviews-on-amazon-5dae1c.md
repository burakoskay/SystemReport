# Introduction to the Problem
Fake reviews are a significant issue on Amazon. A 2019 investigation by Which? found hundreds of tech products from lesser-known brands had fake reviews.
## The Scale of the Problem
The Which? investigation found that popular accessories such as smartwatches and headphones are affected. The problem suggests some companies may be relying on fake reviews to stand out.
## Amazon's Response
Amazon invests resources to protect review integrity, with guidelines for reviewers and selling partners. The company suspends, bans, and takes legal action against those who violate its policies.
## The Impact on Consumers
Fake reviews make it difficult for consumers to make informed purchasing decisions. With many fake reviews, it's hard to know what products are genuinely good.
## Analyzing Professional Product Reviews
Experts have analyzed professional product reviews to calculate a single score. According to 371 experts, updated Mar 25, 2026, and 276 experts, updated Mar 31, 2026, this approach may not be effective in identifying fake reviews.
## Industry Context
The issue of fake reviews is not unique to Amazon. Other e-commerce platforms also struggle with this problem. However, Amazon's large market share and wide range of products make it a significant target for fake reviews.
## History of the Problem
The problem of fake reviews has been ongoing for several years. In 2019, Which? conducted an investigation that found hundreds of tech products with fake reviews. Since then, Amazon has taken steps to address the issue, but it remains a challenge.
## Technical Mechanics
Fake reviews often involve the use of fake accounts and incentivized reviews. These tactics can be difficult to detect, especially for consumers who are not familiar with the signs of fake reviews. Amazon uses machine learning algorithms to detect and remove fake reviews, but the company must continually update its systems to stay ahead of those who try to manipulate the review process.
## Downstream Implications
The impact of fake reviews can be significant. Consumers may purchase products that are not as good as they seem, and legitimate businesses may struggle to compete with companies that use fake reviews to boost their sales. As a result, it is essential for Amazon to continue to invest in its review integrity systems and for consumers to be aware of the potential for fake reviews.
## Market Analysis
Amazon's large market share makes it a prime target for fake reviews. Other e-commerce platforms, such as eBay and Walmart, also struggle with this issue. However, Amazon's size and scope make it a unique challenge.
## Regulatory Environment
Regulatory bodies, such as the Federal Trade Commission (FTC), have taken steps to address the issue of fake reviews. The FTC has issued guidelines for companies to follow when it comes to reviews, and has taken enforcement action against companies that violate these guidelines.
## Consumer Awareness
Consumer awareness is key to combating fake reviews. Consumers must be aware of the signs of fake reviews, such as unverified purchases and overly positive language. By being aware of these signs, consumers can make more informed purchasing decisions and help to reduce the impact of fake reviews.
