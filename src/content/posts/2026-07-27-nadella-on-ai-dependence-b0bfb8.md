## Introduction to AI Dependence
Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella stated that companies trusting one AI for everything may not survive. Nadella's statement highlights the risks of over-reliance on a single artificial intelligence system.

AI adoption is increasing across various industries. Companies without their own models or without AI gateways to separate their prompts from the model itself will be in trouble, according to Nadella. The trend of relying on a single AI system for all operations can lead to significant problems, including loss of control and potential biases in the AI decision-making process.

## The Role of AI Gateways
AI gateways enable companies to separate their prompts from the AI model. This separation is essential for maintaining control over the AI system and ensuring it aligns with the company's goals and values. By implementing AI gateways, companies can ensure that their AI systems are working in their best interests and are not being influenced by external factors.

The use of AI gateways also allows companies to customize their AI systems to meet their specific needs. This can include integrating the AI system with existing infrastructure, ensuring that the AI system is compatible with other systems, and providing a layer of security to protect against potential threats.
