## Google's Mic Drop Gmail Feature Backfires

Google introduced a 'Send + Mic Drop' button in Gmail on April 1. The button sent a Minion-themed GIF and muted subsequent replies. The feature was optional and could be disabled.

## Gmail's April Fools' Prank 
Google's prank was met with backlash from users who accidentally clicked the button, causing embarrassment and annoyance. A freelance journalist reported that the silent send caused their editor to assume negligence, but there is no evidence of immediate termination. This incident highlights the importance of considering user trust when introducing new features, especially those that affect core communication pathways.
