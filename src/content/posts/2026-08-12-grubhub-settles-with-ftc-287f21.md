## Introduction
Grubhub has begun mailing checks to diners and drivers as part of its $23.8 million settlement with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC). This move comes after the company resolved allegations over its business practices, which led to the significant fine.
## Background on the Settlement
The settlement resolves allegations over Grubhub's business practices. Checks are being mailed from Grubhub's $23.8 million fine from the FTC. The FTC's investigation into Grubhub's practices was a crucial step in reaching this settlement, emphasizing the importance of regulatory oversight in the food delivery industry.
## Impact on Diners and Drivers
The checks being mailed to eligible recipients are part of the company's efforts to provide restitution. The FTC settlement stems from allegations over Grubhub's business practices, which had a direct impact on diners and drivers. This impact is not isolated, as it reflects broader issues within the food delivery sector, where companies often walk a fine line between innovation and compliance.
## Industry Context
The food delivery market, where Grubhub operates, is highly competitive and rapidly evolving. However, this growth has also led to increased scrutiny over business practices, with regulators focusing on issues like consumer protection and fair competition. Grubhub's settlement with the FTC is a significant development in this context, as it underscores the need for transparency and compliance in the industry.

The FTC has been actively monitoring the food delivery industry, taking steps to address concerns over business practices.
